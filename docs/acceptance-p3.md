# Phase 3 acceptance

Automated checks: `make test` (includes `tests/test_acceptance_p3.py`) and `make e2e`.
The checks below need real weeks, so they are done by hand.

## Checks

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | On Sunday after 18:00, open Weekly review. | The week's numbers are filled in, and the phone had "Your weekly review is ready". |
| 2 | Answer the four prompts and close the week. Time it. | Done in 20 minutes or less. |
| 3 | After closing, tick a task from that week as done, then reopen the review. | The closed week's numbers have not moved. |
| 4 | On Monday, open This week. | Next week's outcomes from your fourth answer are there as drafts. Choose a milestone for each. |
| 5 | Look at Today. | The direction check shows today's aligned share, learning blocks kept and the planning streak. |
| 6 | Look at This quarter. | Each goal reads on track, at risk or behind, with the pace tick on its bar. |
| 7 | On 24 December, open Today. | The Q1 2027 planning prompt is showing (and the 2027 one, which opened on 1 December). |
